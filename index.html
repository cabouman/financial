Federal Debt: Real Rates, Real Growth, and the Inflation Exit

Debt-to-GDP ratios don't predict debt crises — real rates versus real growth do. Checking that claim (from a Money & Macro video) against the literature and 85 years of U.S. data: the average real rate on the debt is ≈0%, the marginal rate (TIPS, 2.42%) now exceeds forecast growth, and the last exit from a debt peak ran through a decade of negative real rates.
