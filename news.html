July CPI: a Second Quiet Month, and the September Hike Looks Less Likely

Headline CPI rose 0.1% in July and annual inflation edged down to 3.4% — every reading matched consensus. Shelter drove two-thirds of the increase, energy fell again, and September rate-hike odds dropped to 42%.

The July Jobs Report: a Net Loss of Jobs, with Caveats

Payrolls fell 23,000 in July against an expected 83,000 gain — mostly a seasonal quirk in local-government education, but May and June were revised down a combined 103,000 and wage growth slowed to 3.2%, the lowest since May 2021. The report took a September rate hike off the table.
July 14, 2026

June CPI: the largest monthly decline since 2020

Headline CPI fell 0.4% in June — the deepest monthly drop since April 2020 — pulling annual inflation to 3.5% from May's 4.2%. Core eased to 2.6%. Nearly all of it was oil; the Fed still leans toward a September hike.
